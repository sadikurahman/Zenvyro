ABOUT ZENVYRO

Mine. Grow. Earn.

A community-driven rewards platform built around the ZENY coin, designed so anyone with a smartphone can take part.

01Introduction & Welcome

Welcome to Zenvyro. We built Zenvyro around one simple idea: taking part in the digital-asset world should not require expensive hardware, technical expertise or a large upfront payment.

With Zenvyro you start a timed mining session from your phone, claim your ZENY reward when the session ends, and watch your balance grow, one session at a time. Our theme says it all: Mine. Grow. Earn.

Mine

Start a free 4-hour session from any smartphone. No hardware and no technical knowledge needed.

Grow

Build your balance session by session and grow your network with real, active friends through referrals.

Earn

Claim ZENY rewards, then exchange or withdraw through our reviewed request system.

02Our Vision & Mission

Our vision

To open a simple, fair path into the digital-asset economy for everyday people, especially young people looking for an opportunity they can pursue from home with nothing more than a phone and an internet connection.

Our mission

  • Make mining accessible. Every member starts free, with no hardware, no technical knowledge and no upfront cost.
  • Build a strong community. Referral rewards and shared goals recognise members who bring in real, active people.
  • Keep everything clear. Rules, fees and packs are written in plain language, and fees are shown before you confirm an action.
  • Be honest about what we offer. We explain what Zenvyro does and what it does not do, so every member can decide for themselves.

Why we started

Many people want to learn about digital assets but are held back by cost and complexity. Zenvyro lowers that barrier and gives people a place to take their first steps at their own pace.

03Core Values

Transparency

Pack details, session rules and fees are published in the app. The total exchange fee is shown before you confirm.

Security

Withdrawals are reviewed manually before funds are released, and accounts are protected by strong-password rules.

Community First

A free starting pack, referral rewards and a shared community goal keep the focus on our members.

Fair Play

One account per person and one account per device. Bots, emulators, scripts and fake referrals are not allowed.

Our policy against bots and scams is strict and applies to everyone equally. Accounts that break the rules may be limited, suspended or closed, and rewards obtained through abuse may be reversed or forfeited, as described in the Terms & Conditions inside the app.
