01Executive Summary & Project Introduction

Zenvyro is a community rewards platform built around the ZENY coin. It gives anyone with a smartphone and an internet connection a simple way to take part: start a timed mining session, claim a ZENY reward when the session ends, and grow a balance over time.

What Zenvyro offers

  • Free mining for everyone. Every member begins on the Free Pack with no payment required.
  • Optional premium packs. Standard ($10), Silver ($25), Gold ($50) and Platinum ($100) raise the ZENY rate per hour and unlock extra benefits.
  • Referral rewards. Members earn commission from the mining activity of the real people they invite, plus a bonus on a referred member's first pack.
  • Wallet and exchange. Members can hold, transfer and request to exchange ZENY into USDT, BTC, TON, BNB or SOL.
  • Reviewed withdrawals. Every withdrawal request is reviewed and paid manually to protect member funds.

The purpose

Our purpose is to make participation in the digital-asset economy simple, open and clear. We combine free access, a fair set of rules and plain-language communication so that members always know how the platform works.

Current status of ZENY. During Phase 1, ZENY is an in-app balance managed by the Zenvyro platform. Moving ZENY onto a public blockchain network is part of the planned listing stage and is not available yet.

Free to start

Every member can mine from day one on the Free Pack, at no cost.

Any smartphone

Open to everyone

03Phase 1 Mechanics

Phase 1 is the foundation of Zenvyro: a 120-day community mining campaign. A live countdown on the home screen shows how much of the phase remains. Phase 1 is the initial distribution period, designed to build a real, active community before later stages begin.

3.1 Mining sessions and claims

  • Tap the coin to start a 2-hour mining session. You can pause and resume the session at any time.
  • When the session is complete, tap Claim Reward to add the earned ZENY to your balance.
  • Each pack has its own ZENY per hour rate, which is shown in the app.

3.2 Referral program

  • Members who register through your referral link become part of your network.
  • You earn a commission on their mining income, claimed once a day at 10:00 PM Bangladesh time. Unclaimed commission accumulates until you claim it.
  • When a referred member buys their first pack, you receive a purchase bonus.

3.3 Fair use and bot prevention

A fair platform needs fair rules. Zenvyro applies the following:

  • One account per person and one account per device.
  • No automation. Bots, emulators, scripts and fake or ghost referrals are not allowed.
  • The session and claim cycle itself makes automated extraction harder and keeps distribution steady.
  • Manual review of withdrawals adds a human check before any funds leave the platform.
  • Clear consequences. Accounts that break the rules may be limited, suspended or closed, and rewards or balances obtained through abuse may be reversed or forfeited, as set out in our Terms & Conditions.

3.4 Community goal

The dashboard tracks a community goal of 30,000,000 ZENY, which equals the Community Mining & Airdrop allocation described in the next section.

05Future Security & Platform Roadmap

5.1 Manual withdrawal process

To protect member funds and prevent fraud, withdrawals are handled by people, not automatically.

RequestSTEP 1

The member chooses an asset and network, enters their own wallet address and submits a request. The minimum is $10.

ReviewSTEP 2

An administrator checks the balance, the account status and the wallet details.

PaymentSTEP 3

If approved, the payment is sent manually to the member's wallet.

RecordSTEP 4

The transaction is recorded and the request is marked as completed. Rejected requests include a reason.

5.2 Security

  • Strong-password rules and protected accounts.
  • Administrative controls are separate from member accounts, including tools to suspend accounts that break the rules.
  • Before any public listing, we plan to commission an independent security review of the platform.
