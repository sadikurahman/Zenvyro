Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about mining, packs, referrals, withdrawals and the road ahead.

General & Platform Overview

What is Zenvyro?
Zenvyro is a community rewards platform built around the ZENY coin. Members start timed mining sessions, claim ZENY rewards when each session ends, and manage their balance, exchange requests and withdrawals from one account.
How does mining work?
Tap the coin on the home screen to start a 2-hour session. You can pause and resume it. When the timer finishes, tap Claim Reward to add ZENY to your balance. Your pack decides how much ZENY you earn per hour.
Is Zenvyro free to use?
Yes. Every new member starts on the Free Pack and can mine without paying anything. Paid packs are optional.
What is ZENY?
ZENY is the native coin of the Zenvyro platform, with a total supply of 75,000,000. Today you can earn it, hold it, transfer it to other members and request an exchange into other assets. During Phase 1, ZENY is an in-app balance. Wider uses are planned and will be announced when they are available.
Who runs Zenvyro?
Zenvyro is operated by its project team and serves members mainly in South Asia. Official updates are shared only through the official channels listed at the bottom of the app. Zenvyro will never ask for your password.

Account Security & Fair Play

Can I create multiple accounts on the same device?
No. Zenvyro allows one account per person and one account per device. Duplicate accounts may be limited or closed.
What happens if bots or fake referrals are detected?
Bots, emulators, scripts and fake or ghost referrals are not allowed. Accounts found using them may be suspended or permanently closed, and rewards or balances obtained through abuse may be reversed or forfeited, as described in our Terms & Conditions.
Can family members use the same Wi-Fi?
Yes. Family members can use the same Wi-Fi connection as long as each person uses their own device and their own account.
Is my data safe?
We protect account data with encrypted connections and strong-password rules, and we limit access to administrative tools. No online service can promise perfect security, so choose a strong, unique password and never share it. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
Do I need a powerful phone or computer?
No. Zenvyro sessions are timer-based, so you do not need a powerful device, and the app does not drain your battery with heavy processing. Any ordinary smartphone with an internet connection is enough.

Phase 1 & Mining

What is the 120-day Phase 1?
Phase 1 is the first 120 days of Zenvyro, shown as a live countdown on the home screen. It is our community-building and initial distribution period, before the later stages described in the Whitepaper.
Why is there a 2-hour session and claim?
The session and claim cycle keeps reward distribution steady, makes automated abuse harder and encourages regular, genuine participation.
Will my mining rate change?
Each pack has a published hourly rate shown in the app. Zenvyro may adjust rates in the future as the platform develops. Any change will be reflected in the app and in our Terms & Conditions.
How can I increase my mining rate?
Upgrade to a higher pack for a higher ZENY per hour rate, and invite real friends with your referral link to earn referral commission.
What is the 30,000,000 ZENY goal?
The 30,000,000 ZENY goal equals the Community Mining & Airdrop allocation, which is 40% of the 75,000,000 total supply. The progress bar on the dashboard shows how the community is moving toward it.

Premium Packs & Referrals

What are Premium Packs?
Premium Packs are optional upgrades with a higher mining rate and extra benefits. Payments for packs are not used to pay member rewards. They are placed in a separate project fund.
How does the referral program reward me?
When someone registers with your referral link, you earn a commission on their mining income, which you claim once a day at 10:00 PM Bangladesh time. You also receive a bonus when a referred member buys their first pack. Your rate depends on your pack.
Do I earn from inactive referrals?
Commission comes from the mining activity of the members you invite. If an invited member is not mining, no commission is generated from them.
Which packs are available?
The paid packs are Standard ($10), Silver ($25), Gold ($50) and Platinum ($100), in addition to the free starting pack.
Can I buy a pack with my mined balance?
Packs are paid for from outside the app. Paying for a pack with your mined balance is not available at the moment and may be considered in a future update.

Withdrawals, Exchange & Roadmap

What is the minimum withdrawal?
The minimum withdrawal is $10 per request.
Are withdrawals automatic or manual?
Withdrawal requests are reviewed and paid manually by our administrators. This helps prevent fraud and protects member funds.
Which assets and wallets can I use?
In the Wallet section you choose the asset and network (USDT on TRC20, BTC, TON, BNB on BEP20 or SOL) and enter your own wallet address. Always double-check the address and network, because funds sent to a wrong address cannot be recovered.
How does Exchange work, and what is the fee?
You can request to exchange ZENY into USDT, BTC, TON, BNB or SOL. A total 10% fee (3% exchange, 2% network and 5% platform) is shown before you confirm. An administrator reviews the request and the result is sent to your Wallet.
When will ZENY be listed on an exchange?
Public listing is a goal for the stage after Phase 1. No listing date is confirmed and a listing is not guaranteed, because it depends on exchange requirements, compliance review and market conditions. Official announcements come only through our official channels.
Does Zenvyro guarantee the ZENY price?
No. Nobody can guarantee a token price, and Zenvyro does not. Any USDT value shown in the app is for reference only and is not a market price. The total supply is 75,000,000 ZENY, distributed as shown in the Whitepaper.
